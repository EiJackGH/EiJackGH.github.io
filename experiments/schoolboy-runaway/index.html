SCHOOLBOY RUNAWAY

3D First-Person Horror Stealth Lab

"Ah, you scoundrel! Hurry up and study your lessons!" said your dad, locking the bedroom door and putting you under strict house arrest. But your friends are waiting outside!

To escape, you must find a screwdriver, break open the locked drawer to steal the main key, and open the front exit door without getting caught.
Desktop Controls
WASD / Arrows - Walk around
Mouse Drag / Move - Rotate camera
SHIFT - Toggle Crouch (Quiet)
E / Left-Click - Use / Interact
Mobile Controls
Left Joystick - Walk around
Right Drag Zone - Rotate camera
Crouch Button - Toggle Crouch
Use Button - Use / Interact
👁️ Stealth is key: Dad patrols the house. Crouching makes zero noise.
🏺 Distractions: Throw green vases to lure Dad to other rooms.
🚪 Hide: Jump inside Closets or Crawl under Beds when Dad is near.
