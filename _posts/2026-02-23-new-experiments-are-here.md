We are thrilled to announce a massive wave of new interactive experiments now live in the laboratory! From action-packed games to tactical simulations and utility tools, there is something here for every tech enthusiast and gamer.

### Featured Additions:

#### 1. Games & Simulations
*   **Schoolboy Runaway 3D**: Sneak and escape strict house arrest in this fully procedural, stealth horror simulation built in Three.js and Tailwind. Avoid Dad's patrolling flashlight!
*   **Ages of Conflict (World Map Simulator)**: A robust HTML5 Canvas sandbox simulator. Command borders, manage diplomacy, and shape the globe.
*   **I Wanna Be The Guy**: Face retro-platforming trials with pixel-perfect collisions, deadly traps, and relentless difficulty.
*   **WW1 Game**: A historical tactical strategy simulation. Survive the trenches and deploy strategic formations.
*   **Space Invaders**: Retro arcade action built on a fast canvas engine. Fight back the alien waves!

#### 2. Creative Utility Tools
*   **Vexillology Studio (Flag Maker)**: Create gorgeous vector flags with customizable layouts, templates, and dynamic wind-blown physics simulations.
*   **SVG to PNG Converter**: Scale and customize vector assets offline, preview resolutions, and export pristine PNG files.
*   **Video to GIF Converter**: Convert video captures into loopable, lightweight GIFs directly in your browser.
